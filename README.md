# Stanford Law School AI Student Skills

This repository houses the first release of student-facing AI Skills developed for Stanford Law School and the Robert Crown Law Library.

## Core pathway

- SLS AI Orientation
- SLS AI Task-Fit Coach
- SLS Case Learning Coach
- SLS Legal Research Learning Coach
- SLS AI Verification Lab

## Optional tool studios

- SLS Harvey Learning Studio
- SLS Legora Learning Studio
- SLS LexText Learning Studio
- SLS CICERO Oral Argument Studio
- SLS NotebookLM Learning Studio
- SLS AI Tool Explorer

Each downloadable package is a validated `skill.zip` containing its `SKILL.md`, ChatGPT interface metadata, and supporting Stanford Law School references.

## Shared principles

The Skills use a problem-first approach and incorporate the PAUSE Rule, Stanford Law School student AI guidance, Responsible AI at Stanford, productive struggle, legal-source verification, structured and manageable data, transparent AI-use logs, and accurate non-anthropomorphic explanations of AI systems.

For AI tools, access, legal research, or technical assistance, contact the Robert Crown Law Library at **library@law.stanford.edu**.
